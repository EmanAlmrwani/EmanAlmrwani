## Overview

**Plantify** is a mobile UI/UX concept for plant lovers to explore indoor and outdoor plants, learn plant-care tips, browse products, and move through a simple shopping and checkout experience.

|---|---|

| **Color palette** | Green, sage, mint, off-white |
| **Typography** | Plus Jakarta Sans (moodboard) |
| **Status** | UI/UX design concept |

## Design concept

The interface combines a nature-inspired aesthetic with straightforward product discovery and care information. The objective is to make plant exploration feel approachable for beginners while keeping browsing and purchasing flows easy to follow.

## Screen walkthrough

### 01 · Onboarding and sign-in

Splash screen, plant discovery introduction, care tips, growth-focused messaging, and login.

### 02 · Shopping experience

Home page with search and categories, plant product cards, detailed plant descriptions, care instructions, and add-to-cart actions.

### 03 · Checkout flow

Shopping cart, payment options, and successful payment confirmation screens.

## User journey

```text
Splash → Onboarding → Sign in → Explore plants
                                 ↓
                         View plant details
                                 ↓
                            Add to cart
                                 ↓
                         Payment → Success
```

## Design decisions

- **Green and white palette:** a visual connection to growth, nature, and calm.
- **Rounded elements and generous spacing:** a friendly, uncluttered experience.
- **Plant imagery:** makes product discovery visual and engaging.
- **Care information:** supports informed choices beyond product appearance.
- **Clear checkout stages:** separates shopping decisions from payment.

## What this project demonstrates

Mobile screen design, visual identity exploration, onboarding, product discovery, product details, and checkout screen composition.
